## Game Projects

I have made a couple of games that are on my [itch.io](https://seirann.itch.io/)

My game development journey started my passion for coding. The main reason that made me start learning programming was me wanting to make mods for minecraft, so I thought Minecraft is programmed in Java so I need to learn it, but I never made a mod I got too into coding.

The first try at making a game was when I have participated in Ludum Dare 54 with the theme being Limited Space. I arranged a team of 5 people, it was Me and 4 of my class mates, we had an artist, a music guy, me and the other 2 guys as programmers. The idea we had was you have a character that has to run around and collect as many things and drop them in a hole and the level would slowly shrink, so you have less and less space available.

Now this was my first ever game jam and we were very ambitious and we tried to do a lot, but due to just having too big of a project for a 2 day jam we failed to finish the game in time and eventually school homework was catching up to us and we had to give up. It was intense, it was exhilarating and it was fun. The fun and the intense vibe stuck to me, and I promised to myself that I will participate in another game jam.
